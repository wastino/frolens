Street photography is often less about spectacle and more about attention. The strongest images usually arrive when I pause long enough to notice a gesture, a reflection, or a small collision of color and light.

For me, the work begins before the camera comes up. It is a quiet practice of listening to the city, watching for tension, and letting the scene reveal itself rather than forcing it.

The most memorable frames are rarely the loudest ones. They are the moments that feel honest, fleeting, and deeply human.
